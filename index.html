Does any of this sound familiar?

Parenting isn't supposed to feel like you're constantly guessing what your child needs. If you've been feeling worried, exhausted, or unsure how to help, you're not alone. Many families reach out when they've tried everything they know—and are ready for support that helps them understand what's really going on beneath the surface.

Your Child Seems Different

Maybe your once-outgoing child has become withdrawn. Maybe they're anxious, overwhelmed, or no longer enjoying the things they used to love. When something feels "off," it's okay to trust your instincts.

Big Emotions Have Taken Over

Emotional outbursts, shutting down, constant worry, anger, or tears can all be signs that a child is struggling. Together, we'll help make sense of those emotions and build healthier ways to cope.

Home Doesn't Feel Like Home Anymore

When every conversation turns into an argument, everyone is walking on eggshells, or your family feels disconnected, it can be hard to know where to start. Therapy can help restore understanding, strengthen relationships, and bring more peace back into everyday life.

My Approach

I draw from evidence-based, trauma-informed methods and tailor them to each family — because your child isn't a script, and neither is your story.

Trauma-Focused CBT

A structured, gentle approach that helps kids and teens process difficult experiences and build healthier ways of thinking about them.

Emotion-Focused Work

Helping your child name, understand, and ride out big feelings instead of being swept away by them.

Family Systems

Looking at behavior in the context of your family's relationships and home life, not as a problem that lives in your child alone.

Play-Based Interventions

Meeting younger clients where they are, using play as the natural language kids use to process what they can't yet put into words.
