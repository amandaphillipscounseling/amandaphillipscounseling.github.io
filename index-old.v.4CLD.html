Meet Amanda

Amanda Phillips · Associate Professional Clinical Counselor, APCC #14941

Practicing under the supervision of Debbie Ciulla, LMFT #98887

I provide a warm, collaborative, and trauma-informed approach grounded in attachment, humanistic, and family systems principles. I specialize in work with children and teens struggling with big emotions, anxiety, depression, or behavioral challenges — and with parents feeling exhausted by recurring patterns at home.

"Every behavior tells a story."

Whether your child is melting down, withdrawing, or struggling to cope, my goal isn't simply to stop the behavior — it's to understand the emotions and unmet needs beneath it, so lasting change can happen.

Christian counseling is available for clients who would like it.

How I Can Help

Children

Anxiety, emotional regulation, trauma, confidence, and coping skills, using play-based and age-appropriate tools.

Teens

Depression, identity, relationships, school stress, and the transitions that come with growing up.

Families

Strengthening communication, connection, and understanding between parents and kids.

My Approach

I draw from evidence-based, trauma-informed methods and tailor them to each client — never a one-size-fits-all script.

Trauma-Focused CBT

A structured approach that helps children and teens process difficult experiences and build healthier thought patterns.

Emotion-Focused Work

Helping clients name, understand, and regulate big feelings instead of being overwhelmed by them.

Family Systems

Looking at behavior in the context of relationships and home dynamics, not in isolation.

Play-Based Interventions

Meeting younger clients where they are, using play as a natural language for processing emotion.
